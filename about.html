What do we mean by site, program and app?

Site

A website is a group of pages you open in a browser using a link, like a company page, an online store or a portfolio. It works on any device and needs no installation.

Program

A program is software installed on a computer to do a specific job, like accounting, inventory or managing a clinic. It can often work without internet.

App

An app is a mobile application you download on your phone (Android or iPhone). It is designed for touch screens and can send you notifications.
